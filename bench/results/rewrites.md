# Rewriting: costex vs Daisy

Both rewriters get the same seed, and both are scored by measuring error in bits rather than bounding it: 10000 uniform points of the core's box, the same ones for every program of that core, plus the worst points a hill climb reaches from them -- pooled, so every program is scored at every other program's worst case as well.  That makes the score a lower bound on the worst case -- but neither rewriter optimises measured error, so neither can game it.  For bounds, see `bounds.md`.

A verdict needs a **2.0x** margin in ulps.  Resampling with independent point sets moves the ratio by 1.0x at the median and 1.75x at the 90th percentile, so a smaller gap is not distinguishable from having drawn different points, and is reported as too close to call.

- 260 of 371 cores get a verdict; the 106 tagged optimal are left out, as are 5 where neither side produced a program that could be measured
- of those 260, **84 have headroom**: the seed loses more than 4 ulps, so there is something for a rewriter to take off
- sampling sample-12, 10000 points drawn uniformly over the floats of each box, seed 20250827, then 8 climbs per program over a ladder of 64 step sizes
- daisy 7544e92, seed 1490, `--analysis=dataflow --rangeMethod=interval --errorMethod=affine --rewrite --codegen --lang=FPCore`
- daisy: 347 ok, 15 crash, 5 timeout, 2 unparsed, 1 nooutput, 1 unsupported, and returned the seed unchanged on 174

## Summary

Only a core whose seed is inaccurate can be made more accurate.  The corpus is mostly seeds that are already right to a couple of ulps, and on those every rewriter ties by construction, so the headline is the cores with headroom:

- **on the 84 cores with headroom: costex wins 21, daisy wins 2, dead tie 33, too close to call 28**
- **bits of error taken off the seed, mean over those cores: costex +2.82, daisy +1.69**

How that moves with where the bar is put:

|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|

The whole corpus, headroom or not, for completeness:

- costex wins 29, daisy wins 3, dead tie 176, too close to call 52
- a side whose program could not be measured loses: 17 of costex's wins and 0 of Daisy's
- of the 176 dead ties, 34 are cores where both rewriters returned the same program, so there was nothing to separate; the other 142 returned different programs that measure identically
- the 52 too close to call differ by less than the 2.0x margin
- daisy ulps / costex ulps, 243 both measured: geomean 1.4x, median 1x, p10 1x, p90 1.5x

Each rewriter against the seed it was given, same points:
